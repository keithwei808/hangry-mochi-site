Hangry Mochi

Run and walk with your crew.

Running and walking habits last when you do it with people you care about and who notice. Hangry Mochi turns you and up to 9 friends into a small crew that shares one Mochi pet. Every run or walk, by anyone in the crew, feeds it.

Your goal, your pace. Everyone sets a weekly goal that fits them.

Run together from anywhere. Live runs show your whole crew side by side.

Cheers that reach you mid-run. Friends tap to cheer or send a short voice cheer.

Hangry Mochi is invite-only for now, on iPhone.

Hangry Mochi

Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Hangry Mochi is a running and walking app for small crews of friends, made by Kirk Wei in Washington, USA. This page explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. In short: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and the first name you choose. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple may share a private relay email instead of your real one.
  • Runs and goals: distance, time, pace, the date of each run, your weekly goal, badges and records.
  • Location, only during a run: your phone's GPS measures distance while a run is in progress, including when the screen is locked. We store the starting point of a run (to award race medals) and, during Live runs and race day, your current distance so your crew and fans can follow along. We don't track your location when you aren't running.
  • Your survey answers: answers to the short questions about your running habits. They are private to you and used to suggest a weekly goal.
  • Things you create: crew names, Mochi names, cheers, and voice cheers. Voice cheers are short audio clips (up to 10 seconds); each clip is deleted after it plays.
  • Safety reports: if you report someone, we keep the report (who, why, and any note you add) so we can review it.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a push token so we can send them.

We don't collect contacts, photos, health records, advertising IDs, or browsing history, and we don't use analytics or advertising tools.

Who can see what

  • Your crewmates: your name, weekly goal, runs and distances, and cheers in the crew.
  • Fans of your crew (people who entered your crew's fan code): runner names, Live runs and race day distances.
  • Nobody else in the app: your email and survey answers are never shown to other people.
  • Service providers who run the app for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Expo (app updates and delivering notifications) and Apple (Sign in with Apple, notifications, app distribution). They process data only to provide these services.

We may share information if required by law or to protect someone's safety.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while you have an account. Voice cheers are deleted after they play. When you delete your account, we delete your profile, runs, records, badges, goals and survey answers. Food your runs already fed a crew's Mochi stays with that Mochi, and safety reports may be kept to protect other users.

Your choices

  • Delete your account anytime in the app: Me → Delete my account.
  • Change your name or answers on the Me page.
  • Turn off location or notifications in your iPhone's Settings. Without location, runs can't measure distance.
  • Block someone so their cheers never reach you: Me → Help & feedback.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or to delete it: hangrymochiapp@gmail.com.

Children

Hangry Mochi isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app for important changes.

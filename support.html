Report or block someone

In the app, open Me → Help & feedback, tap the person, and choose Report or Block. You can also tap a crewmate on Home, then Report or block. We review every report within 24 hours. Blocking is private: the other person isn't told, and their cheers and voice cheers no longer reach you. If you started the crew, you can also remove a member or fan.

If someone is in danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Common questions

My distance isn't counting. Allow location access for Hangry Mochi (Settings → Hangry Mochi → Location → Always) and run outdoors. GPS is weak indoors and under heavy cover.

How do I join a crew? Ask a friend in the crew for its code and enter it on the first screen. Fans enter a fan code instead.

How do I delete my account? In the app, open Me → Delete my account. Your profile, runs and goals are deleted.
